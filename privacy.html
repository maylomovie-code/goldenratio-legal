Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

This policy explains how the Golden Ratio Tips mobile app ("the app") handles your information. The app is published by Golden Ratio Tips (the "controller"). Contact: aidevelopx@gmail.com.

Information we collect

  • Account information: your email address, an optional display name, and the basic profile data shared by Google or Apple when you sign in with them, plus a user ID.
  • Purchase information: which package you bought, the transaction ID and date. Payments are processed by the App Store or Google Play; we never see your card details.
  • Device identifier: identifierForVendor on iOS or ANDROID_ID on Android, used only to prevent abuse and duplicate fake accounts.
  • Notification token: used to send push notifications (via OneSignal). You can turn notifications off in your device settings.

How we use it

To create and manage your account, deliver the credits and premium content you purchase, prevent fraud and abuse, and send notifications about new tips. We do not use your data for advertising or cross-app tracking, and we do not sell it.

Service providers

  • Google Firebase – authentication and database
  • OneSignal – push notifications
  • Apple App Store / Google Play – payments
  • API-Football – public match fixtures and scores (no personal data is sent)

Retention and deletion

Your data is kept while your account is open. You can permanently delete your account and data in the app under Menu → My Profile → Delete Account, or by following the steps on our account deletion page. Sales records that we are legally required to keep may be retained for the required period.

Age requirement

The app is intended only for users aged 18 and over.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (e.g. GDPR in the EU, KVKK in Türkiye), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your data. Write to aidevelopx@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days.

Disclaimer

Predictions are for information and entertainment only; no winnings are guaranteed. The app does not accept bets and does not link to betting services.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version.
